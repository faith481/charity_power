🎯 Our Mission

To eliminate dark nights in underserved communities by providing subsidized and free electricity recharge credits, ensuring that every household has access to basic lighting, safety, and power for daily living.

👁️ Our Vision

A society where energy poverty is eradicated, where children can study safely at night, and where socioeconomic standing does not dictate access to basic electricity infrastructure.

Our Core Commitments

Direct Distribution

100% of collected meter numbers are verified and credited directly through official power distribution networks.

Transparency

Verification processes ensure that relief funds reach genuine, less-privileged beneficiaries.

Community Focus

Prioritizing low-income families, elderly citizens, and rural households without steady income.
